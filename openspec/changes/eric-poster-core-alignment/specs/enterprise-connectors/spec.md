## Purpose

Provides enterprise data ingestion management across six multi-channel sources matching the poster requirements.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Support 6 enterprise connector channels
The system SHALL ingest and monitor status for 6 distinct data channels: Documents, Emails & Communications, Reports & Dashboards, ERP/CRM Systems, Spreadsheets & Databases, and Employee Knowledge.

#### Scenario: View connector status
- **WHEN** user navigates to the Sources view
- **THEN** the system displays all 6 data channel cards with active status, last sync timestamp, and record counts.

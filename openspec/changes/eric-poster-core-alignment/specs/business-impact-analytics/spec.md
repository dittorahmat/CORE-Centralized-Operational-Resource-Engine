## Purpose

Tracks and quantifies executive value metrics including hours saved, productivity gains, decision accuracy, and compliance.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Poster business impact metrics
The system SHALL present 5 key business impact cards: Save Time, Better Decisions, Increase Productivity, Protect & Retain Knowledge, and Improve Compliance.

#### Scenario: View business impact summary
- **WHEN** user selects the Impact view
- **THEN** all 5 value pillars are rendered with live metrics, key performance indicators, and calculation breakdowns.
